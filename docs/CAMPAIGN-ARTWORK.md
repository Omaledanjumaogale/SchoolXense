# SchoolXense campaign artwork

Six original assets were generated with Codex's built-in ChatGPT image generation tool. The tool did not expose a model-version selector or report the specific model revision. The source PNGs are retained in the generated images folder; the application ships optimized WebP copies in static/artwork.

The prompt set called for believable Nigerian settings, editorial lighting, mobile-safe framing, and no embedded copy, logos or watermarks. The three card prompts depicted evening study in Lagos (indigo and amber), a campus maths tutoring breakthrough (burgundy and copper), and an outdoor peer study session (teal and gold). The distinct wide promotional prompts depicted focused flashcard practice (indigo and amber), two learners working through a problem (plum and coral), and a peer workshop (emerald and gold), with dim space on the left for live HTML typography.

The scenes illustrate fictional experiences. They are not customer photographs or testimonials.
